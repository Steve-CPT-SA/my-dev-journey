From Electrician to Developer

I have 12 years of experience as an electrician, and now I'm building a daily coding habit to transition into tech with a focus on AI/ML development.

Career Transition FAQ

Why electrician -> tech?

What began as a hobby quickly grew into a passion. As I explored coding, I realized it aligned with my problem-solving mindset and opened new opportunities. Over time, my focus shifted from electrical work to tech, and I now dedicate time every day to learning and building.

What challenges do I face?

Balancing a full-time, physically demanding job with consistent study requires discipline. Even when I'm tired after work, I make space in my evenings and weekends to practice. Rather than seeing age as a disadvantage, I view my experience and persistence as strengths that fuel my transition.

What resources help the most?

FreeCodeCamp is giving me a strong foundation, while Coddy.tech helps me practice daily. I also use mobile apps like Mimo and Enki during mornings before work. For deeper understanding, I turn to Claude and Copilot to clarify concepts and guide me through challenges when needed.

Where am I headed?

My immediate goal is to step into a junior developer role, knowing that growth comes from starting at the ground level and building upward. I'm excited to continue this journey, expand my skills, and ultimately specialize in areas like AI and machine learning.
